# **App Name**: FacturaFlow

## Core Features:

- User Authentication & Roles: Secure login and distinct access permissions for Owner, Administrator, Cashier, and Accountant user roles.
- Branch Management: Allow Owners and Administrators to add, view, edit, and activate/deactivate multiple business branches.
- Customer Management: Create, edit, and view customer profiles including contact and billing information associated with specific branches.
- Product/Service Catalog: Maintain a catalog of products and services, allowing specification of pricing, applicable taxes, and inventory levels (if needed for an MVP).
- Invoice Generation & Tracking: Enable cashiers to create new invoices, add line items from the product catalog, apply discounts, generate PDF copies, and track their payment status.
- Basic Sales Reporting: Provide Owners and Accountants with basic summary reports on sales performance, revenue by branch, and outstanding invoices.
- Sales Trend Assistant (AI): Utilize an AI tool to offer concise summaries and identify potential trends in sales data across branches, assisting Owners and Accountants in quick decision-making.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A professional deep blue (#335C99) conveying trust, stability, and efficiency in a business context. Chosen for its balance and authority, suitable for a light-themed interface.
- Background color: A very light, desaturated blue-grey (#EEF1F6) derived from the primary hue (hsl(220, 15%, 95%)). This ensures a clean, unobtrusive canvas that promotes readability of forms and data, aligning with a light color scheme.
- Accent color: A vibrant cyan-blue (#1AC4E6) (hsl(190, 80%, 50%)) used for highlighting important actions, call-to-action buttons, and key interactive elements. Its brightness and distinct hue provide excellent contrast and draw attention effectively, enhancing user guidance.
- 'Inter' (sans-serif) will be used for all text, including headlines and body content. Its modern, neutral, and highly readable design ensures clarity across numerical data, forms, and reports, supporting the system's focus on efficiency and accuracy.
- Utilize a consistent set of crisp, line-art icons that are clear and intuitively represent business functions such as branches, invoices, users, and reports.
- Implement a structured, responsive layout with clear sectioning and ample white space to ensure high scannability of financial data and forms. Prioritize user efficiency in data entry and information retrieval.
- Subtle, non-disruptive animations will be used to provide immediate feedback for user actions like form submission, data loading, or successful saving, enhancing the perceived responsiveness of the application.
